Use ML and remote sensing to map crops, predict yields, detect pests and diseases, and guide variable-rate precision agriculture.

When the user is working on or asking about `ai for agriculture`.

1. Load the `ai-for-agriculture` skill for the full reference.

4. Point them at the references and any relevant `cuda-blackwell-labs` or `agent-skills` examples.
