## Publishing & Peer Review

### Cover letter (journals)
- 1 page: why this journal, the contribution in 2 sentences, confirmation of originality/no dual submission, suggested reviewers (no collaborators).

### Rebuttal protocol
1. Thank reviewers; summarize changes up front in a table: concern → response → where changed.
2. Respond to every point, even trivial ones; quote the reviewer verbatim before answering.
3. New experiments beat arguments. If a reviewer requests an ablation and it takes 6 hours, run it.
4. Concede gracefully when wrong: "The reviewer is correct that X; we revised to Y."
5. Tone: never defensive. Assume the reviewer missed it because the draft was unclear.

### Reviewer ethics (when you review)
- Confidentiality: never share, upload to third-party tools without permission, or use ideas before publication.
- Timely, evidence-anchored reports; judge the paper against its own claims.
- Declare conflicts (same institution, recent collaboration).

### Venue mechanics
- Track deadlines (abstract vs full paper often differ by a week).
- Double-blind: anonymize refs to your own work as third person; strip metadata/PDF properties/acknowledgments.
- Resubmission: address every prior comment in a point-by-point letter even years later.
