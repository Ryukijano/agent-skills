# Autonomous Research Orchestration

## Two-Loop Architecture

### Inner Loop: Experiment Optimization
1. Generate hypothesis
2. Design experiment
3. Run experiment (training, evaluation)
4. Analyze results
5. Refine hypothesis
6. Repeat until convergence

### Outer Loop: Research Synthesis
1. Literature survey (search, read, summarize)
2. Identify research gap
3. Formulate research question
4. Route to domain skills for experiments
5. Synthesize findings
6. Write paper sections

## Integration
- Routes to `literature-search-*` for paper discovery
- Routes to `experiment-tracking` for logging
- Routes to `ml-paper-writing` for publication
- Routes to `academic-plotting` for figures

## Principles
- Continuous operation until quality threshold met
- Human checkpoints at major decisions
- All experiments logged and reproducible
- Incremental paper writing
