# Research Brainstorming

## Ideation Frameworks

### 1. Gap Analysis
- Read 10 recent papers in the area
- List what each does NOT address
- Cluster gaps to find themes

### 2. Cross-Domain Transfer
- What techniques work in domain X?
- Can they apply to domain Y?
- What adaptations are needed?

### 3. Inverse Problem
- What is the ideal outcome?
- Work backwards to what's missing

### 4. Scale Analysis
- What breaks at 10x scale?
- What assumptions no longer hold?

### 5. Failure Analysis
- Where do current methods fail?
- Why do they fail?
- What would fix it?

## Evaluation Criteria
- Novelty: Is this new?
- Impact: Does anyone care?
- Feasibility: Can I do it?
- Clarity: Can I explain it in one sentence?

## Output
- 5-10 candidate ideas with one-paragraph descriptions
- Ranked by novelty x feasibility
- Top 3 expanded into half-page proposals
